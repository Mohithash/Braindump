# Braindump

**Get it out of your head.** A bring‑your‑own‑key AI task planner.

- 🧠 **Brain dump** — type (or snap a whiteboard/notebook) everything on your mind → clean tasks with priority, resolved due dates ("Friday" → a date), project, estimate; non‑actionable thoughts kept as notes; ambiguities surfaced as questions. Tick what to keep.
- 📅 **Plan today** — a realistic time‑blocked schedule from your open tasks within your working hours, with lunch, buffers and 20% slack; tells you what to defer and why.
- ✅ **Today** — overdue, due‑today and urgent tasks in one focus list; progress per block.
- 🗂️ **Tasks** — quick add, filter by project, clear done.
- 🔎 **Weekly review** — completions chart plus a kind, honest read: what went well, what slipped, one change for next week.

BYOK: Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI‑compatible endpoint. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
